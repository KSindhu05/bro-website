# Praveen ❤️ From Your Akka Sindhu

> "ನನ್ನ ಪ್ರೀತಿಯ ಅಣ್ಣ... ಈ website ನಿನಗಾಗಿ ಮಾತ್ರ ಮಾಡಿದ್ದು, ಪ್ರೀತಿಯಿಂದ ನಿನ್ನ ಅಕ್ಕನಿಂದ ❤️"

A cinematic, interactive surprise web experience created with infinite love by **Sindhu** for her beloved brother **Praveen**.

## ✨ Features
- 🎬 **10-15s Cinematic Movie Trailer Intro**: 3 emotional fullscreen photos with slow-motion Ken Burns zoom, cinema letterbox bars, and glowing titles.
- 📸 **Our Story Photo Timeline**: Cherished moments and memories with bilingual Kannada & English captions.
- 🔢 **Animated Counters**: "365+ Days of Memories", "1000+ Laughs Together", "500+ Precious Photos", and "∞ Love From Your Akka".
- 💌 **Sindhu's Actual Handwritten Letters**: Authentic handwritten diary letters and the hilarious **10 Golden Bond Rules ("Lifetime Subscription Activated", "No Cancellation Allowed")** with an interactive lightbox zoom modal.
- 🎙️ **Voice Messages**: Real voice messages with animated sound waves and progress tracking.
- 💫 **3D Rotating Memory Gallery**: Interactive 3D carousel and slideshow.
- 🛡️ **Akka's 5 Reasons & Promises**: Why Praveen is the best brother in the world.
- 🎉 **Grand Finale Surprise**: Heart explosion, floating hearts, and fireworks confetti.
